# NetArchitect Privacy Statement

**Last updated: September 2026**

## The short version

NetArchitect is an offline-first network design tool. It has **no accounts, no analytics, no network access, and all data stays on your device**. We cannot see your plans because we never receive them.

## What NetArchitect does

NetArchitect is a subnet calculator, VLSM allocator, network planner, and security auditor. It runs entirely on your device and works completely offline.

## What data NetArchitect collects

**None.** NetArchitect does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, usage analytics, or network information.

## Where your data lives

- **Saved plans** are stored in a local SQLite database on your device.
- **Preferences** (theme, input format) are stored in AsyncStorage on your device.
- **Learning progress** (practice attempts) is stored in AsyncStorage on your device.

All data remains on your device. There is no cloud sync, no backup server, and no remote storage.

## Network access

NetArchitect **never connects to a network**. It has no `fetch` calls, no HTTP clients, and no analytics SDKs. The app functions identically in airplane mode.

## Permissions

NetArchitect requests only the permissions necessary for its core functionality:

- **File system access** — to save and export configuration templates.
- **Clipboard access** — to copy calculation results and configuration templates.

No location, camera, microphone, or contact permissions are requested.

## Third-party services

NetArchitect uses no third-party services, no advertising networks, and no crash reporting SDKs.

## Children's privacy

NetArchitect does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Since the app collects no data at all, there is no risk of collecting children's information.

## Changes to this statement

If this privacy statement changes, the update will be noted with a new date above. Since NetArchitect collects no data, changes are unlikely.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy statement, please open an issue on the NetArchitect GitHub repository.
